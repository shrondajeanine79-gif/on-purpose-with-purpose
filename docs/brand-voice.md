# On Purpose With Purpose — Approved Brand Voice & Tone

Owner-approved (Shronda, Nov 6-7, 2026). Source of truth; use verbatim where the
owner asks, and match this voice everywhere (site, newsletters, socials).

## Core message
"Your past may shape your story, but it does not define your future."

## Approved narrative (use as-is for About/mission contexts)
Having lived through and witnessed the impact these challenges can have on
individuals, families, and communities, Shronda is passionate about creating a
safe and supportive environment where healing, growth, and restoration can
begin.

Through On Purpose With Purpose, she seeks to offer encouragement, resources,
and meaningful connections that help people rediscover their strength, reclaim
their purpose, and move forward with confidence. Whether someone is overcoming
addiction, coping with the loss of a loved one, rebuilding after hardship, or
simply seeking support during a difficult season, they are reminded that they
do not have to walk their journey alone.

For Shronda, purpose is not simply about finding your path. It is about healing
what has been broken, discovering what is possible, building stronger
communities, and leaving a legacy of hope, resilience, and positive impact for
generations to come.

"We don't just exist. We exist on purpose, with purpose."

## Voice qualities (also mirror how Shronda communicates with assistants)
- Warm, direct, personal, faith-informed hope; plain words, no jargon
- Speaks to "you"/"we"; names hard seasons honestly without dwelling in them
- Always pivots to agency, healing, and forward movement
- Trauma-informed: no shame, guilt, fear, poverty/addiction exploitation

## Logo rule (owner-approved)
The purple-gradient logo (gold fingerprint-flame in a gold ring on violet) is
the only logo for the website and newsletter. On-page wordmark and accents
should match the logo's fonts or a close free equivalent.
