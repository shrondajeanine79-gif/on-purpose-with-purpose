Meet

Shronda

Founder of On Purpose With Purpose

Shronda is an author, inspirational speaker, community advocate, and the visionary behind On Purpose With Purpose—a community dedicated to personal growth, emotional wellness, and purposeful living.

Get the Free GuideGet in Touch

Grounded in lived experience, Shronda understands that healing isn’t linear. Her own journey shaped her commitment to emotional awareness and intentional living, fueling her passion to help others reclaim their voices and unlock their potential.

Through On Purpose With Purpose, she blends reflection, education, creativity, and wellness. Her ultimate vision is to create a welcoming sanctuary where people—particularly Black women whose experiences deserve to be seen and heard—can find encouragement, meaningful resources, and genuine connection.

For Shronda, purpose is about what we restore within ourselves, what we build together, and the positive impact we leave behind.

Our foundation

What we stand on

Our Mission

To empower individuals through emotional wellness, self-discovery, and authentic connection while encouraging intentional growth and meaningful collective action.

Our Vision

A world where people recognize their inherent worth, embrace their authentic identities, and contribute to stronger, more compassionate communities.

Our Purpose

To create a restorative space where reflection leads to growth, growth strengthens connection, and connection inspires positive change.

What guides us

Our Core Values

1

Authenticity

Honoring who we are without the pressure to perform or pretend.

2

Self-Worth & Empowerment

Reclaiming our voices, recognizing our value, and making intentional choices.

3

Restoration & Wellness

Prioritizing emotional well-being, mindfulness, rest, and healing.

4

Connection & Belonging

Building relationships rooted in respect, compassion, and shared humanity.

5

Education & Awareness

Fostering critical thinking, emotional intelligence, and informed decisions.

6

Advocacy & Community Action

Supporting civic engagement, mutual aid, and collective responsibility.

7

Purposeful Growth

Turning internal reflection into outward action and lasting impact.

Our guiding philosophy

Restore. Grow. Rise.

We believe that when individuals have the space to restore themselves, they are empowered to grow, connect, advocate, and help their communities rise.
