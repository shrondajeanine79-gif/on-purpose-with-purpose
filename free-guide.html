8 Ways to Be Good to Yourself

A gentle guide to giving yourself the same care you so freely give everyone else. Eight small practices, each with one action you can take today.

What’s inside

Small on purpose.

  • Set boundaries that protect your energy
  • Talk to yourself like you’d talk to a friend
  • Prioritize rest without justifying it
  • Move your body in ways that feel good
  • Create small moments of pleasure
  • Let go of productivity guilt
  • Seek help when you need it
  • Forgive yourself for being human

By Shronda, author of The Power of Words.
